## Conventions

- `BB1:c41` = board BB1, column c, row 1-63.  Columns a-e and f-j are separate 5-hole strips.
- `BB1:L+@12` = left + rail next to row 12 (`L-`, `L+`, `R+`, `R-`).  Rails are assumed split between rows 31 and 32; the listed bridge wires join the halves.
- `SW:`, `LED:`, `PWR:` = soldered pads on the switch panel, LED panel and power fixture; one pad per net.  `PWR:VCC` / `PWR:GND` are the regulator output and return.
- Red wire for VCC, black for GND.  Lengths are straight-line estimates with 15 % slack + 10 mm.
